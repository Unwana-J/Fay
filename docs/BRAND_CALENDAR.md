## 1. Core Strategy: "Parlor in December → Study in January"

1. **December = Parlor Season (The Trojan Horse):** People want fun at Christmas dinners, office end-of-year hangouts, Detty December link-ups, and family group chats. Introducing Fey through **The Podium**, **Articulate**, **Naija Trivia**, and **Quant** builds real usage with zero friction.
2. **Early January = Study Season (Public Beta Launch):** On **Tuesday, Jan 5, 2027** (right as people return to desks setting *"read more, think deeper, sound sharper"* habits), launch with the 65-second film and refreshed post-holiday user count: *"You played Fey with friends over Christmas. Now make it your 15-minute daily thinking habit."*

---

## 2. Subtle Brand Signature (70 / 30 Rule) & The "Launch Vault"

- **70% genuine founder perspective** (learning, career clarity, articulation, Richard Feynman, honest building-in-public stories from [`docs/DECISIONS_LOG.md`](./DECISIONS_LOG.md)).
- **30% direct Fey drops** (15s soft-launch teaser, live game challenges, holiday party packs, Jan 5 launch film).
- **The Launch Vault (Anti-Repetition Rule):** Never recycle the exact lines or puzzles reserved for the 65s January Launch Film in pre-launch cards or the 15s teaser. Keep these saved for Jan 5:
  - *"1,000 posts read / 240 bookmarks / 40 tabs"* Receipt
  - *"Feeling informed isn't the same as knowing."*
  - Feynman's *"What I cannot create, I do not understand."*
  - Mathletics `[2, 3, 4 → 20]` / `[4, 5, 6 → ?]` · Trivia *Hakeem Olajuwon 1994* · Podium *"Nigerian Customer Service is a Spiritual Test"*
- **Visual Consistency:** Every branded card uses warm cream paper (`#F7F5F0`), forest olive (`#444E2C`) framing, wine red (`#7A1C2E`) accents, antique gold (`#A67C1E`) rewards, Lora serif headlines, zero emojis, and a quiet Fey butterfly + `fey.lokinlabs.com.ng` colophon.
- **Default Export Formats:** `linkedin` (`1200×1500` portrait 4:5 — also reusable for IG in Jan) + `status` (`1080×1920` full-screen 9:16 — native for WhatsApp Status and TikTok/IG Stories).
